Frequently Asked Questions
I Dare You To Ask…

Will it be comfy?

Very. The DuraFlow™ blend of hemp, bamboo, and organic cotton at 190 GSM creates a lighter, stretchier polo than you’re used to. The honeycomb knit adds natural flex and airflow. Soft against the skin, fitted to enhance the male physique — grips arms and chest, sits loose where it needs to. Comfortable from the first wear and gets better with every wash.

How can it be lighter AND still durable?

Hemp is doing the structural work — 51% of the strongest natural fiber on the planet. The honeycomb knit distributes tension across more contact points, like a bridge. The open structure is what makes it strong, not solid mass. 30% lighter than our tees. Same lifetime warranty. We wouldn’t guarantee it forever if it couldn’t handle it.

What makes it odor-free?

Bamboo and hemp both possess natural antimicrobial properties that fight odor at the fiber level. No chemical treatment. No spray-on coating that washes out. Combined with the honeycomb air pockets that wick moisture and let it evaporate, you stay dry and fresh. All natural performance.

What’s so unique about DuraFlow™?

Crafted with DuraFlow™ tech (patent-pending) — Earth’s first hemp-blend performance polo. Honeycomb knit for faster drying, natural stretch, and superior airflow. Without a single thread of plastic.

The tri-blend features a three-strand structure, stronger than two-strand because it provides even tension, and more stable than four-strand where fibers can conflict.

For the polo, we took it further with honeycomb knitting — same blend, different stitch — creating air pockets for faster drying, more stretch, and better airflow. The perfect balance of strength, durability, and performance. Designed for comfort. Built to last.

What’s with the coconut shell buttons?

Every button on the polo is carved from real coconut shell. Not plastic. Not petroleum. You can feel the natural grain. Each one is slightly different because they’re carved from actual shell, not stamped from a mold. It’s the kind of detail you’d find on a $200 polo. Zero plastic anywhere on this product.

What is honeycomb knit?

Same DuraFlow™ blend as our tees but woven with a honeycomb stitch that creates tiny air pockets in the fabric. This gives the polo faster drying, more stretch, and better airflow at 190 GSM — 30% lighter than our tees. Same durability. Same lifetime warranty.

Is it really for performance golf or pickleball?

Built for it. 190 GSM honeycomb knit gives you full range of motion — golf swing, pickleball serve, tennis rally — without restriction. Faster drying means no sweat patches by the back nine. Lighter fabric means no heavy-shirt fatigue. Engineered for the course, the court, and the cookout after.

Lifetime guarantee — really?

Yes, really. Our polos are built to last a lifetime. If your UndrDog polo is the wrong size or ever wears out, we’ll replace it with a brand new one, free of charge. All you need to do is cover the small cost to send it back to us, and we’ll handle the rest. That’s our commitment to quality.

What’s UndrDog’s big vision?

To redefine the way men think about clothing. We’re committed to creating the strongest, most comfortable shirts on the planet that don’t sacrifice health or performance. Our mission is to provide men with a lasting solution to cheap fast fashion, offering stylish shirts that stand the test of time. The polo is the next chapter.
