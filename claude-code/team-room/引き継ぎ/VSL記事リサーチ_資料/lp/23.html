FAQs

  • Why consider IM8 during perimenopause or menopause?

    IM8 brings a broad range of daily nutrients into one drink, with support for energy metabolism, normal neurological function, bones, joints and digestion. It may suit you if you want to simplify your supplement routine. It is a daily nutrition supplement, not a treatment for menopause or a promise to resolve every symptom.

  • Were the trial results specifically measured in menopausal women?

    Menopause-specific results are not provided in the trial summary presented here. The figures describe participants reporting outcomes in a 12-week randomised controlled trial. They should not be read as proof that IM8 treats menopause-related sleep problems, brain fog or other symptoms.

  • Does IM8 balance hormones or replace HRT?

    IM8 is a nutritional supplement. It is not presented as hormone therapy, a treatment for hot flushes, or a replacement for HRT or prescribed care. Discuss symptoms and treatment options with your clinician.

  • Can I take it alongside my current supplements or medication?

    Share the full ingredient list with your clinician or pharmacist before adding it to your routine, especially if you use HRT, other medication or prescribed supplements. This page does not establish compatibility with an individual treatment plan. Compare amounts before combining supplements.

  • What will I pay, and can I cancel?

    The 90-serving subscription is $235 every 12 weeks ($2.61 per serving). The 30-serving subscription is $89 every 4 weeks ($2.97 per serving). A 30-serving one-time purchase is $112. Subscriptions renew automatically; pause or cancel anytime. Each first subscription order includes a free welcome kit.

  • What if it isn’t right for me?

    Quarterly subscriptions include a 90-day money-back guarantee. Monthly subscriptions and one-time purchases include a 30-day money-back guarantee. Contact care@im8health.com to request a refund.

  • How do I take it, and what does it taste like?

    Mix one single-serve sachet with water and enjoy as part of your daily routine. Choose Açaí + Mixed Berries, Lemon + Orange, or Mango + Passion Fruit. The Variety Pack includes 10 sachets of each flavour in a 30-serving supply.
