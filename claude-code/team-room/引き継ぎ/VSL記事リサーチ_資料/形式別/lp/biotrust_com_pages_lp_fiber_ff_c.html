Frequently asked questions

I'm on a GLP-1 medication – is this for me?
Yes. GLP-1 medications often help you feel fuller sooner and eat less. That can support your weight goals, but eating less usually means less fiber, widening a gap most people already have. Ageless Multi Fiber was designed for exactly that situation: six fibers, including a clinical dose of Solnul® resistant starch, gentle enough to take every single day.
What is Ageless Multi Fiber?
Ageless Multi Fiber is a next-generation, healthspan-focused fiber supplement designed to help fill two of the most overlooked nutritional gaps in modern diets: dietary fiber and resistant starch. Each serving delivers 6 grams of dietary fiber from a strategically designed blend of soluble fiber, insoluble fiber, resistant starch, and naturally occurring polyphenols.

Powered by clinically studied Solnul® resistant potato starch and supported by complementary fibers, Ageless Multi Fiber supports digestive health, regularity, gut microbiome balance, metabolic health, and healthy aging.

Rather than relying on a single fiber source, Ageless Multi Fiber delivers a diversified fiber system designed to support the body through multiple complementary mechanisms.
What Does Ageless Multi Fiber Do?
Ageless Multi Fiber helps support digestive health, regularity, gut microbiome diversity, metabolic wellness, and healthy aging by delivering multiple types of fiber that perform different functions within the body. The formula works by:

• Feeding beneficial gut bacteria
• Supporting healthy microbial balance
• Promoting digestive regularity
• Supporting healthy stool consistency
• Providing fermentable substrate for short-chain fatty acid (SCFA) production
• Supporting gut barrier function
• Helping promote satiety and fullness
• Helping fill widespread fiber and resistant starch gaps

Unlike many fiber products that focus on a single mechanism, Ageless Multi Fiber supports the entire gut health cascade—from prebiotic activity to postbiotic production to digestive regularity.
What are the ingredients in Ageless Multi Fiber and what do they do?
Ageless Multi Fiber contains six carefully selected ingredients that provide complementary forms of fiber and gut health support:

• Solnul® Resistant Potato Starch: A clinically studied type 2 resistant starch that acts as a prebiotic fiber. Solnul® has been shown to support beneficial bacteria such as Bifidobacterium and Akkermansia, healthy stool patterns, gut barrier function, nutrient absorption, and metabolic health.
• Partially Hydrolyzed Guar Gum (PHGG): A gentle, soluble, fermentable fiber that supports digestive comfort, regularity, and microbiome balance.
• Acacia Gum: A well-tolerated soluble prebiotic fiber that helps nourish beneficial bacteria while supporting digestive health.
• Oat Fiber: A predominantly insoluble fiber that supports stool bulk, intestinal transit, and healthy bowel function.
• FIBRISS® Organic Baobab Fruit Powder: Provides soluble fiber, insoluble fiber, and naturally occurring polyphenols that support microbiome diversity and digestive wellness.
• Citrus Pectin: A soluble, fermentable fiber that complements the microbiome-supporting benefits of the formula and contributes additional polyphenols.

Together, these ingredients provide a diversified fiber system designed to support digestive and overall health from multiple angles.
How quickly will I see benefits?
The timeline can vary depending on the individual and the benefit being evaluated. Many people notice improvements in digestive regularity and stool consistency within the first several days of consistent use. Benefits related to the gut microbiome may take longer to develop because beneficial bacteria need time to respond to changes in fiber intake.

For best results, Ageless Multi Fiber should be taken consistently every day as part of a healthy diet and lifestyle.

Will Ageless Multi Fiber make me bloated?
Ageless Multi Fiber was intentionally formulated to be gentle and well tolerated. Many fiber supplements rely heavily on a single fiber source or rapidly fermenting fibers, which can sometimes lead to bloating, gas, or digestive discomfort—especially when consumed in large amounts or introduced too quickly. Ageless Multi Fiber takes a different approach by combining:

• Clinically studied Solnul® resistant potato starch
• Partially hydrolyzed guar gum (PHGG)
• Acacia gum
• Oat fiber
• Baobab fiber
• Citrus pectin

These fibers were selected not only for their benefits, but also for their digestive tolerance.

In clinical research, Solnul® resistant starch was shown to be well tolerated and did not increase reports of:

• Bloating
• Gas
• Abdominal discomfort
• Belching
• Nausea

That said, anytime fiber intake is increased, some individuals may experience temporary digestive changes as the gut microbiome adapts. For those who currently consume very little fiber, starting with a partial serving and gradually increasing to a full serving may help improve tolerance.
Can Ageless Multi Fiber help with occasional constipation?
Yes. Fiber is one of the most important nutrients for supporting healthy bowel function and regularity. Ageless Multi Fiber contains multiple types of fiber that support healthy digestion through complementary mechanisms:

• Insoluble fibers help provide stool bulk.
• Soluble fibers help support digestive wellness and microbial balance.
• Resistant starch helps nourish beneficial gut bacteria and support healthy stool patterns.

Clinical research on Solnul® resistant starch demonstrated improvements in constipation-associated stool patterns, helping support more normal bowel function.

For best results, Ageless Multi Fiber should be used consistently and consumed with adequate fluid intake.
Are there any side effects?
Ageless Multi Fiber is generally well tolerated when used as directed. As with any fiber supplement, increasing fiber intake may lead to temporary digestive changes in some individuals, particularly those who currently consume very little fiber. Potential temporary effects may include:

• Increased bowel activity
• Changes in stool frequency
• Mild gas
• Mild bloating during the adjustment period

These effects are typically temporary and often improve as the digestive system adapts. The fibers in Ageless Multi Fiber were selected in part because of their favorable digestive tolerance profile compared to many traditional fiber supplements.

How should I take Ageless Multi Fiber?
Mix one serving daily with water or your favorite cold beverage and consume as directed. Ageless Multi Fiber can be taken at any time of day. Many customers prefer taking it in the morning as part of their daily wellness routine, but consistency is more important than timing.

We do not recommend mixing Ageless Multi Fiber into hot beverages or foods that exceed approximately 140°F (60°C). Ageless Multi Fiber contains resistant starch, which is valued for its ability to resist digestion in the small intestine and reach the colon intact.

Exposure to high temperatures may alter the structure of resistant starch and reduce some of its unique prebiotic properties and benefits. For best results, mix Ageless Multi Fiber into cold or room-temperature beverages, smoothies, yogurt, or other cool foods.
