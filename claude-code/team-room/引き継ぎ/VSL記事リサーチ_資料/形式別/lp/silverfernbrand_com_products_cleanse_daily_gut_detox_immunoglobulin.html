WHY DO WE NEED IMMUNOGLOBULINS?

When a mother is breast feeding, she delivers high levels of immunoglobulins to the baby. These immunoglobulins are essentially protein antibodies that help a baby fight off harmful pathogens. Breast fed babies tend to have fewer ear infections, respiratory issues, and bouts of diarrhea.*

Antibodies are the body’s most important and abundant soldiers for keeping us safe from foreign invaders.* Immunoglobulins are specialized protein antibodies that bind like a lock-and-key to bad stuff in the gut. This is the body’s search and destroy system for fighting off pathogens while supporting immune health.* Cleanse has one of the highest concentrations of the most robust immunoglobulins you can take for amazing gut support.*
