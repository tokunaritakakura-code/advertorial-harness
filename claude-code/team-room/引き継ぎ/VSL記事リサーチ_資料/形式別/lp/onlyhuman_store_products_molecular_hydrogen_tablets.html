    Drop one tablet into 12-16oz of room-temperature water, wait about 2 minutes for it to fully dissolve, and drink immediately. Repeat this process one to three times daily.
    What does it taste like?
    A light, slightly fizz, most people find it clean and easy to drink. No sweeteners, no flavoring, and no aftertaste.
    When will I notice a difference?
    Some people notice better energy and recovery within days, but support builds with consistency. It is suggested to give it 2-4 weeks of daily consistent use.
    Why tablets instead of hydrogen water bottles or machines?
    Hydrogen machines cost hundreds of dollars and require maintenance. Molecular Hydrogen Tablets deliver both a high concentration and consistent dose of Hydrogen in any glass of water.
    Are there any side effects?
    Molecular Hydrogen is generally very well tolerated. Each tablet also contains magnesium, which some people are sensitive to in high amounts. Start with one tablet daily.
