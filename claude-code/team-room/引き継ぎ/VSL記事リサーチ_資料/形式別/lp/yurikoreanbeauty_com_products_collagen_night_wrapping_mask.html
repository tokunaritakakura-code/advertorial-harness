    How To Use?

    1. PREP YOUR SKIN

    Cleanse, then follow your routine up to toner or serum. Clean, hydrated skin boosts absorption.

    2. BRUSH IT ON

    Use the applicator brush to spread an even layer over your face. Avoid brows, hairline, eyes, and lips.

    Let it set for about 15 minutes, then head to bed. The veil works with your skin's natural overnight renewal cycle.

    4. PEEL & GLOW

    In the morning, gently peel from the edges in one piece, or rinse with lukewarm water. Use 2-3 nights a week for lasting results.

    Your Questions About Glass Skin, Answered

    Everything you need to know about achieving effortless overnight radiance with our Collagen Night Wrapping Mask.

    How exactly does the wrapping effect work?

    After applying, the mask sets into a thin, breathable veil that seals in moisture and your skincare while you sleep. This overnight seal supports continuous 8-hour hydration. In the morning, it peels away in one clean piece.

    How often should I use the mask for best results?

    For a consistent glass-skin glow, use it 2-3 nights a week. The low-irritation, dermatologist-tested formula is gentle enough for more frequent use if your skin wants an extra boost of hydration.

    Can I use it with my existing skincare routine?

    Yes. It is designed as the final step of your nighttime ritual. It locks in your favorite serums and moisturizers, helping your current products work even harder overnight.

    Will it clog my pores or cause breakouts?

    The formula is low-irritation, dermatologist tested, and free from artificial colorants and fragrances. The veil is breathable, so skin is not suffocated overnight, and balanced hydration can help reduce the excess oil that contributes to breakouts.

    Is the mask supposed to set into a film?

    Yes, that is the wrapping effect working. About 15 minutes after applying, the cream sets into a flexible, comfortable film you sleep in. In the morning, gently peel it away from the edges or rinse with lukewarm water.

    How long before I see a real difference?

    Most customers notice smoother, more hydrated skin after the very first night. In self-test results, measured improvements in elasticity and hydration appeared within 2 weeks of consistent use.

    What if I don't see the glow results?

    You are covered by our 60-day money back guarantee. If your skin doesn't look noticeably smoother and more radiant, contact us for a full refund. No questions asked.
