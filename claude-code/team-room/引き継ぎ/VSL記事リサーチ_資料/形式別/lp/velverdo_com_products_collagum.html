    How It Works

    Your gums are 90% collagen. It's the scaffolding that keeps them thick, dense, and anchored to your teeth.

    After 30, your body produces less collagen every year. By 50, you're making 70% less than in your twenties. That's why gums thin, pull back, and recede — even with perfect hygiene.

    CollaGum delivers Tropocollagen Type I directly to your gumline. It's small enough to actually penetrate the tissue — not just sit on the surface like regular collagen.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    What is CollaGum?

    CollaGum is a collagen-based powder you brush onto your gums. It helps support gum repair, strength, and comfort — right where your smile needs it most.

    How does it work?

    Your gums are made mostly of collagen. Over time, brushing, stress, and aging can break that collagen down.

    CollaGum delivers fresh collagen and minerals directly to your gums as you brush, helping to rebuild and strengthen the tissue.

    How do I use it?

    After brushing your teeth, dip your damp toothbrush into the CollaGum powder. Gently brush it onto your gums and along the gumline for 20–30 seconds, then rinse or leave it on overnight — whichever you prefer.

    When will I see results?

    Many people notice healthier-feeling gums within the first week. With daily use, gum fullness and strength become more visible in 4–8 weeks.

    Do I still need to floss or see my dentist?

    Yes — CollaGum supports gum health but doesn’t replace good dental care. Keep brushing, flossing, and visiting your dentist as usual for the best results.

    Can I use CollaGum with regular toothpaste?

    Yes! Use your normal toothpaste first, then apply CollaGum right after. It’s designed to fit easily into your regular brushing routine.
