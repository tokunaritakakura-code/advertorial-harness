The Science Behind It

Magnesium is involved in over 300 biochemical reactions in your body, from sleep and stress regulation to muscle function and nerve health. Yet most people are deficient without even knowing it.

But not all magnesium supplements are created equal. Cheap forms like magnesium oxide are poorly absorbed and notorious for causing digestive discomfort.

That's why we use magnesium glycinate, a chelated form bound to the amino acid glycine. This pairing is clinically shown to deliver superior absorption while being exceptionally gentle on the stomach. Glycine itself promotes relaxation, making this the ideal form for sleep, stress, and recovery. Every capsule is optimally dosed to ensure you get real, noticeable results

Is It Safe?

Yes, our magnesium glycinate supplement is 100% natural, non-GMO, vegan, and made without any artificial fillers or preservatives. All ingredients are third-party tested for purity and potency in a GMP-certified facility.

Magnesium glycinate is one of the best-tolerated forms of magnesium and is unlikely to cause digestive issues. However, if you're pregnant, nursing, or taking medication (especially blood pressure or heart medications), we recommend consulting your healthcare provider before use, just to be safe

What's the difference between 1800mg and 384mg on the label?

1800mg is the total weight of the magnesium compounds (glycinate, malate, and citrate combined). 384mg is the elemental magnesium — the actual magnesium your body absorbs and uses. Think of it like this: the compound is the vehicle, the elemental magnesium is the passenger. The 384mg is what matters, and it's what you should compare across brands

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does this contain three forms of magnesium instead of just glycinate? Each form serves a different purpose. Magnesium glycinate (1000mg) is the star — it's the most bioavailable and gentle form, ideal for sleep and stress. Magnesium malate (400mg) supports energy production and reduces muscle fatigue. Magnesium citrate (400mg) promotes healthy digestion and overall magnesium levels. Together, they give you broader, more complete support than any single form alone.

Will this cause diarrhea or upset my stomach? Very unlikely. Magnesium glycinate is specifically known for being the gentlest form on the stomach — unlike cheap magnesium oxide or citrate, which are notorious for causing bloating and loose stools. Our formula is led by glycinate for this exact reason. If you've had bad experiences with other magnesium supplements, this is the form for you.

How much should I take per day? Take 2 capsules once per day. This delivers 1800mg of total magnesium from three premium forms, providing 384mg of elemental magnesium — well within the recommended daily range for most adults. No complicated dosing, no need for extra capsules throughout the day.

Should I take it in the morning or before bed? If you're taking it primarily for sleep, take it 30–60 minutes before bed. For general wellness, stress, or muscle recovery, morning or evening both work well. You can also split the dose — one capsule in the morning, one before bed — though most of our customers prefer taking both capsules together at night.

How do I know this is real magnesium glycinate and not buffered with oxide?Great question — many brands label their product as "glycinate" but secretly bulk it up with cheap magnesium oxide to inflate the numbers. Our formula is transparently labeled with the exact amounts of each form. What you see on our Supplement Facts panel is exactly what's inside the capsule. No hidden fillers, no bait-and-switch.

How long until I feel a difference?It depends on what you're taking it for. Muscle cramps and tension can improve within a few days. Sleep quality typically improves within 1–2 weeks. Stress and mood benefits usually emerge around 2–4 weeks. For full results, we recommend giving it at least 4–6 weeks of consistent daily use. Magnesium is not a sleeping pill, it's a mineral your body needs to build back up over time.

Is magnesium glycinate the same as magnesium bisglycinate?Yes — they are chemically identical. "Bisglycinate" is the technical name (two glycine molecules bonded to one magnesium atom), while "glycinate" is the common shorthand. You may also see "diglycinate" — same thing. Don't pay a premium for a fancier name.

Can I take this with vitamin D, zinc, or other supplements?Yes. Magnesium and vitamin D are a perfect pair — your body actually needs magnesium to activate vitamin D. Take calcium separately (2–4 hours apart) as they compete for absorption. Same with iron — space them out by at least 2 hours. Melatonin pairs safely with magnesium and can enhance its sleep benefits.

Will this interact with my medications?Magnesium can interact with certain medications, particularly antibiotics (take 2 hours before or 4–6 hours after), thyroid medication (separate by 4+ hours), and blood pressure drugs. Most interactions are managed through timing, not avoidance. If you're on prescription medication, we recommend consulting your doctor before starting — just to be safe.
