    How To use

    1. Cleanse & Dry
    Apply to clean, dry skin for optimal absorption.

    2. Target, Don’t Saturate
    Use a small amount on forehead, smile lines, jawline, and neck.

    3. Massage Upward
    Gently massage upward and outward until the silk peptide threads fully melt.

    Always lift — never drag downward.

    4. Use 1–2x Daily
    Immediate smoothing. Visible firmness with consistent use.

    Consistency + upward application = maximum lifting effect.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    How quickly will I see results?

    Most people feel a tightening effect within minutes of application - that’s the silk peptide threads forming a temporary lifting network on the skin.

    Visible improvements in fine lines, smoothness, and firmness typically appear within 7 days.

    For more dramatic changes (lifted jawline, softened deep wrinkles, more defined facial contour), use it consistently for 30 days.

    Is Navaro a Korean Brand?

    No. Navaro is a Black-owned skincare brand.

    While we take inspiration from Korean skincare innovation, our brand was created with a different mission—to provide high-performance skincare specifically for melanin-rich skin.

    We combine proven skincare science with the unique needs of melanin-rich skin to create products that deliver visible results.

    Is this really comparable to Botox or fillers?

    This is not an injectable or a replacement for medical procedures.

    It’s formulated with powerful “Botox-like” peptides (including Acetyl Hexapeptide-8), a 9-peptide complex, ultra-low molecular collagen, and Volufiline™ to help relax the look of expression lines, improve skin density, and visibly smooth and lift the skin — without needles.

    Think of it as a needle-free, at-home alternative for visible everyday results.

    What are those gold threads I see in the bottle?

    Those are silk peptide threads infused with active ingredients.

    They look like tiny gold threads in the formula. Once applied, they melt on contact with your skin and create a temporary micro-lifting network while delivering peptides and collagen exactly where wrinkles form.

    It’s inspired by professional thread-lift procedures - but in a gentle, needle-free serum.

    Is it safe for sensitive skin and all skin types?

    Yes, it’s designed to be gentle and suitable for all skin types, including sensitive skin.

    The formula is lightweight, non-irritating, and free from harsh actives that commonly trigger sensitivity. It uses skin-friendly peptides, ultra-low molecular collagen, and a silk-water base to support hydration and smoothing without redness or heaviness.

    As with any skincare product, we always recommend doing a small patch test first, especially if you have very reactive skin.
