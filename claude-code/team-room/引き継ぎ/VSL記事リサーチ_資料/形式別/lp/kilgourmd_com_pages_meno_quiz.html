        Why Your Answers Point To A Faster-Ageing Scalp
        Your level
        ​
        MildModerateElevatedAdvanced

        18 years ahead of your age of 55

        Scalp age is a measure of follicular capacity — how much of your hair the scalp can hold in growth, how reliably it restarts growth after a shed, and how thick a strand it can still build. Yours is showing its age in all three.

        Marker 1Shedding Phase More shedding

        The extra shedding you’re noticing? That’s your scalp age pushing hair out of anagen — the growth phase — and into telogen, the shedding phase, too early.

        Studies show a young scalp keeps about 9 in 10 hairs in anagen at once; an aged scalp drops to 3 to 5 in 10.

        Source: Follicular Miniaturization in Female Pattern Hair Loss — Royal Hallamshire Hospital, Sheffield

        Marker 2Empty Follicles Less regrowth

        The reason your part is where it shows most: an aged scalp stops keeping up with what it sheds. A hair falls out, and instead of a new one starting behind it, the follicle just sits there empty.

        Studies found up to a third of follicles sitting empty like this for months at a time — dermatologists call them kenogen, and a landmark paper named them the main cause of thinning.

        Source: Rebora & Guarrera, Dermatology; Guarrera & Rebora, Skin Appendage Disorders

        Marker 3Thick vs Fine Hair Less thickness

        And the hair that does grow back comes in finer: an aged scalp builds thin strands where it once built thick ones.

        Studies show the ratio drops from about 13 thick hairs for every fine one down toward 2 to 1.

        Source: Follicular Miniaturization in Female Pattern Hair Loss — Royal Hallamshire Hospital, Sheffield

        How KilgourMD Helps Reduce Shedding

        Anagain™

        Hair Cycle Active

        The first job of a young scalp is holding hair in growth. As it ages, that grip fails — the signal telling a follicle to keep growing fades, the signal telling it to stop takes over, and more hair gets pushed out of growing and into shedding.

        AnaGain works on both sides of that. It turns the growing signal back up and quiets the one telling follicles to stop. In its own clinical studies, it nearly doubled the number of hairs in the growing phase.

        Procapil™

        Anti-Shedding Peptide

        While AnaGain resets the cycle, Procapil works on grip. It reinforces the anchoring proteins at the base of the follicle, so healthy strands stop letting go before their time. In its studies, it cut hair fall by up to 46%.

        6–12 months ago — You started

        Hair is only ever doing one of two things: anagen, growing without interruption, or telogen, where it stops growing and sheds out later.

        Studies show a GLP-1 shifts the balance — hair that should have stayed in anagen moves into telogen instead. The calorie deficit pushes more across at the same time. A healthy scalp holds about 90% in anagen. On a GLP-1 it can fall to 40–50%.

        3–6 months ago — You noticed it

        The first hairs that shifted finished telogen and let go — that’s what showed up in your brush and your shower drain. But that was only the first batch. You were still on your GLP-1, so more kept shifting behind them.

        3–6 months ago — You came off

        Most women are told the shedding settles once the GLP-1 is out. That’s true of what hadn’t shifted yet — nothing new gets pushed into telogen after you stop.

        It isn’t true of what already had.

        Today — It’s visible

        Every month on your GLP-1, another batch moves into telogen and sheds out. And the follicles they leave behind stay empty — nothing’s pushing them back into anagen while you’re still on it.

        So it builds both ways. Which is why your part typically keeps widening — more falling out each month, and less coming back to fill what already went.

        Ahead — What the research shows

        A follicle can sit in telogen a while and still come back. Past a point, it stops re-entering anagen at all.

        You said you’re staying on your GLP-1 as long as it takes. That’s the right call for your health — but it keeps shifting hair into telogen the whole time, and more of it crosses over while it does — and the longer it runs, the more of it there is.
