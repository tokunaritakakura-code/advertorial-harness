The Science Behind It

Frequently Asked Questions

How long until I see results? Most users report noticeable improvements within 2 to 4 weeks of daily use—especially in joint stiffness and overall mobility. Some feel relief sooner, but consistent use is essential.

What’s the correct dosage and how should I take it? Follow the label—typically 3 capsules daily. For best results, take with food (ideally something with healthy fat) to improve absorption, since curcumin is fat-soluble.

How is this better than regular turmeric powder? Most turmeric powders contain just 2–6% curcumin, making them too weak to be effective. Our supplement uses a 95% standardized extract and adds black pepper extract for superior results.

Are there any side effects? Turmeric is safe for most people. A few may experience mild stomach upset—this can usually be avoided by taking it with food. High doses may cause issues, so always follow directions.

Is it safe to take with medications? If you’re on blood thinners, diabetes medication, or have health concerns, speak with your doctor before using turmeric. It may enhance or interact with certain medications.

What makes this turmeric supplement different? Unlike many brands that use weak powder, we use 95% curcumin extract, include black pepper extract for absorption, and verify quality through rigorous third-party testing. No fillers, no fluff—just real results.
