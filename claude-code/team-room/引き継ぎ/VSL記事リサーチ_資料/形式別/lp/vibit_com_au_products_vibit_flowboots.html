How can Vibit FlowBoots Help You?

Vibit FlowBoots combine thigh-high compression with soothing heat to relieve swelling, speed recovery, and refresh tired legs - giving you lighter, stronger, pain-free movement in just minutes a day.

Vibit FlowBoots speed lymphatic drainage by using sequential, wave-like air pressure to mimic muscle contractions, gently squeezing lymph fluid and metabolic waste (like lactic acid) from the limbs upwards toward the core for processing, reducing swelling and muscle soreness by flushing out toxins and improving circulation without physical exertion.

Vibit FlowBoots relieve edema and swelling by using intermittent pneumatic compression(IPC) to create a wave-like massage, sequentially inflating and deflating chambers from the feet upward, which enhances lymphatic drainage and venous return, flushing out excess fluid, metabolic waste (like lactate), and reducing fluid buildup in tissues, mimicking the body's natural muscle pump to promote healing and reduce heaviness.

Vibit FlowBoots improve blood circulation by using air-filled chambers that sequentially inflate and deflate, creating a "milking" or massage effect that pushes deoxygenated blood and metabolic waste (like lactic acid) up the legs towards the heart, while allowing fresh, oxygenated blood to flow in.

Vibit FlowBoots deliver a warm, soothing massage-like experience, offering therapeutic relief for those on their feet all day or battling tired, sore legs - leaving you refreshed and revitalised.
