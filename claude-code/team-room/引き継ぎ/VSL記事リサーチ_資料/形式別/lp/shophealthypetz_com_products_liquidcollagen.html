    The Science Behind It

    Your dog's skin has a protective barrier made of collagen — and it's breaking down faster than you think.

    Dogs lose 7-10% of their collagen every year after age 2. Genetics, chronic scratching, antibiotics, and poor diet can accelerate it even faster. That's why some 2 or 3-year-old dogs already have chronic skin issues.

    As collagen depletes, gaps form. Allergens penetrate deep. The immune system goes into overdrive. That's the constant itching, hot spots, and scratching that never stops.

    Apoquel and Cytopoint suppress symptoms — but they don't rebuild the barrier. That's why they fade.

    Our formula rebuilds the barrier itself. All three collagen types (Types I, II & III) in liquid form for 98% absorption. Plus Omega 3 for fast itch relief and Vitamin C to help your dog's body turn that collagen into new, healthy skin tissue.
