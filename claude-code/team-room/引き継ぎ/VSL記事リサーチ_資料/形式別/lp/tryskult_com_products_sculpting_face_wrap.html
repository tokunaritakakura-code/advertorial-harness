When will I start seeing a more defined jawline?
Most users notice an immediate reduction in puffiness and a temporary lift after the first 20-minute session. Significant, more permanent structural lifting typically occurs within 4 to 8 weeks of consistent use.
What is 300 Dalton collagen and why is it better?
Standard collagen molecules are too large to penetrate the skin's surface. 300 Dalton is an ultra-low molecular weight that is clinically proven to pass through the dermis, delivering actual structural repair where you need it most.
Is it safe to use after cosmetic procedures?
Yes, many customers use it after chin lipo, facelifts, or thread lifts for added support. Always follow your surgeon’s recommendations and ensure your skin has healed enough before wearing.
Is it uncomfortable to wear for long periods?
Not at all. The wrap is made from a soft, breathable, medical-grade fabric designed to feel like a gentle hug for your jaw. Most women use it while relaxing, watching TV, or working from home.
Can I use it with my skincare products ?
Absolutely! You can apply your favorite serums or creams before wearing the wrap — it helps lock in hydration and enhances absorption.
Does it specifically target double chins and jowls?
Absolutely. The wrap is engineered to target the lower face and submental area specifically, counteracting gravity and providing the necessary compression to tighten sagging jowls.
Is it reusable?
Yes — each wrap can be used up to 15 times. Easy to clean, quick to dry, and ready for your next session.
What sizes are available?
The wrap is one-size-fits-most thanks to its fully adjustable Velcro design. It comfortably adapts to different face shapes and head sizes.
