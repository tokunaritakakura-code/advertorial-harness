    Why Every Ingredient Is Here

    Every single thing in this formula has one job: to protect your eyes and keep them working their best. Nothing is in here just to fill space. Each ingredient was chosen because it does something real — something you'll actually feel and see.

    Lutein
    Think of it like a shield that sits right in front of your eye. It filters out the harsh light that wears your vision down over time. The result? Clearer, sharper sight — so the words on a page actually look like words, not a blur.
    Zeaxanthin
    Your eyes take a beating from screens, phones, and bright lights every single day. Zeaxanthin steps in and absorbs that harmful blue light before it can do damage. That means less strain, less headache, and eyes that still feel good at the end of the day.
    Meso-Zeaxanthin
    This one guards the very center of your vision — the part you use to read, recognize faces, and see fine detail. It cuts down on glare and makes colors and edges look sharper. When this part of your eye is healthy, the world just looks cleaner.
    Astaxanthin
    Your eyes have tiny muscles that work hard all day. Astaxanthin helps them recover and keeps the blood flowing where it needs to go. Less tired eyes. Less that heavy, burning feeling after a long day of reading or screen time.
    Vitamin C
    Free radicals are basically tiny troublemakers that attack your cells — including your eye cells. Vitamin C hunts them down and stops them. Healthy eyes stay healthy longer. Simple as that.
    Vitamin E
    Same idea as Vitamin C, but Vitamin E works inside the cells themselves. It wraps around your eye cells like a bodyguard. The longer your cells stay protected, the longer your vision stays strong.
    Alpha-Lipoic Acid
    This one does something pretty cool — it actually helps recharge your other antioxidants so they keep working longer. It also looks after the nerves that carry signals from your eyes to your brain. Healthy nerves mean your brain gets a clear picture, not a fuzzy one.
    Omega-3 Fatty Acids
    Dry, scratchy, irritated eyes? That's usually a moisture problem. Omega-3s keep your eyes properly lubed up and your tears doing their job. No more gritty feeling. No more blinking fifty times just to see straight.
    Zinc
    Zinc helps your eyes handle low light. It supports the back of your eye where night vision happens. Driving after dark, finding your seat in a dim room, navigating a parking lot at night — zinc helps you do all of that without squinting and guessing.
    Copper
    Copper works alongside Zinc to keep things balanced. It also powers the enzymes your body uses to fight damage in your eyes. Think of it as the quiet support player that makes everything else work better.
    Bilberry Extract
    People have used bilberries for eye health for a long, long time — and there's a reason. This extract strengthens blood flow in your eyes and makes night vision sharper. Seeing clearly in the dark isn't just convenient. It's safety.
    Saffron Extract
    The light-sensitive cells in your eyes are some of the most important cells in your whole body. Saffron protects them and helps keep your vision sharp and detailed. The kind of sharpness that lets you read small print without holding it a foot from your face.
    Ginkgo Biloba Extract
    Your eyes need good blood flow to stay healthy. Ginkgo gets that blood moving and fights off the damage that slows it down. Better circulation means your eyes get the nutrients they need — and they get to keep doing their job for years to come.
    Luteolin
    Inflammation is one of the sneakiest ways eyes break down over time. Luteolin blocks that process right at the source. It's quiet protection — the kind you don't notice until you realize your eyes still feel great ten years from now.
    Taurine
    Light is actually hard on your eyes over a lifetime. Every bright day, every glowing screen — it adds up. Taurine wraps around your retinal cells and shields them from that damage. It's one of the reasons some people keep sharp vision well into old age.
