Vitis Vinifera (Grape) Seed Oil, Cannabis Sativa Seed Oil, Rosmarinus Officinalis (Rosemary) Leaf Oil, Mentha Piperita (Peppermint) Oil

1. Rich in Nutrients: With oils infused with Omega 3, 6, and 9 fatty acids, our hair growth stimulant provides essential nutrients that nourish the scalp and support optimal blood flow to hair follicles and promote healthy hair growth from the roots.

2. Calms Irritation: Known for its soothing properties, peppermint oil can calm scalp irritation and inflammation, providing relief while the scalp is being stimulated for hair growth.

3. Eliminates Dandruff: With its antimicrobial and anti-inflammatory properties, rosemary oil helps eliminate dandruff by combating the underlying causes of flakiness and itchiness, leaving the scalp healthier and dandruff-free.

4. Strengthens Hair: Antioxidants and vitamin E, help to fortify your strands with our stimulator, which strengthens hair from root to tip, restoring natural shine and vibrancy.

5. Suitable for All Hair Types: Our formula is gentle yet effective, making them suitable for all hair types, including damaged and color-treated hair. 

Perform a patch test the day before by applying a small amount to another area of your skin.

Apply directly to the scalp and/or edges in sections and massage thoroughly to distribute evenly for optimal absorption and stimulation. Ensure thorough coverage of your entire scalp during application to maximize results. Suitable for daily use. For optimal results, use at least 3–4 times per week. Do not rinse.

Warning: For external use only. Avoid contact with eyes. If irritation occurs, discontinue use. Keep out of reach of children.
