    What is PDRN?

    PDRN 'Polydeoxyribonucleotide', is a powerful skin-repairing compound derived from salmon DNA.

    A molecule studied in Korean aesthetic clinics for it's proven regenerative and anti-inflammatory properties—used primarily for wound healing, post-surgical recovery, and advanced skin rejuvenation treatments.

    PDRN is known for its ability to:

    • Stimulate fibroblasts (the cells that produce collagen)
    • Improve tissue regeneration
    • Calm inflammation
    • Accelerate healing from within

    We are proud to be one of the first to make this clinically-proven ingredient available as an at-home micro-infusion treatment, so you can get the benefits without the clinic hassle.

    How do I use it?

    Using your ELIXA PDRN Micro-Infusion Booster is simple and designed for at-home convenience:

    1. Prep your skin – Start with a freshly cleansed face to ensure the serum absorbs properly.

    2. Fill the device – Pour the PDRN Renewal Serum into the micro-infusion device chamber. Leave the device upside down for 2-3 minutes to ensure the serum enters the needle head.

    3. Apply – Gently press the device onto your skin. The ultra-fine needles create tiny channels, delivering the serum deep into the skin for maximum absorption. Work evenly across your face, focusing on areas with fine lines, dark spots, or uneven texture.

    4. Finish – Follow with your usual moisturizer or sunscreen.

    Each session takes about 5-10 minutes, and for best results, we recommend using it once every 2 weeks.

            Frequently Asked Questions

            When will I see results?

            Most users notice smoother, more hydrated skin and a healthy glow within 24–48 hours of their first ELIXA session.

            For deep acne scars, visible improvement takes consistency — most see meaningful scar fading and smoother texture after 4-6 sessions (about 2-3 months).

            Think of it as “collagen training”: each session stimulates fresh, healthy skin to gradually replace old scar tissue.

            Is it painful? Does it hurt?

             Our Micro-Infusion Skin Booster uses ultra-fine 24K gold-plated microneedles thinner than a human hair to penetrate the uppermost layer of skin.

            Most people describe the sensation as a light tingling or pinprick, and experience zero pain.

            Are there side effects or downtime?

            The ELIXA system is designed to be safe and gentle for at-home use. Some mild redness, light swelling, or a tight feeling may appear in the first 24 hours — a normal sign of collagen activation.

            Rarely, light flaking may follow. These effects subside quickly.

            To avoid irritation, we recommend pausing strong actives (like retinol, acids, or Vitamin C) for 24–48 hours post-treatment and focusing on hydration and SPF.

            What should I do after each treatment?

            After each treatment, your skin is in “recovery mode” — and also more receptive to anything you apply. To protect your results and avoid irritation, we recommend the following:

            First 24 hours:

            - Avoid makeup, sun exposure, and sweating (no gym, sauna or steam)
            - Do not apply active ingredients like retinol, acids, scrubs, or exfoliants
            - Keep skin clean and hydrated using a gentle, fragrance-free moisturizer
            - Try not to touch your face, especially with unwashed hands

            Day after:

            - Wash your face with a mild cleanser, pat dry, and moisturize
            - If you go outside, apply a high-quality, non-comedogenic SPF

            What’s normal:

            It’s common to experience light redness, dryness or a tight feeling for a few hours. Some users may notice slight flaking or peeling a few days later — a sign your skin is renewing.

            Pro tip:

            Your skin absorbs ingredients up to 3x better after infusion, so avoid applying anything harsh during this window. Stick to calming, barrier-repair formulas only.

            Most users return to their full skincare routine within 48 hours — but you may not even feel the need for much. The serum does most of the work.

            How often can I do the treatment?

            For best results on acne scarring, we recommend one ELIXA session every 2 weeks. This schedule allows your skin time to regenerate collagen in between.

            Consistency is key: most users see progressive improvements after 4–6 sessions, with continued benefits if maintained long-term.

            Can I use it on my neck, lips or other areas of the body?

            Yes — it can be safely used on the neck, lips, and most areas of the body where you want to improve skin texture, fade scars, or stimulate collagen.

            Many users apply it also to areas with:
            - Post-acne scars on the back or chest
            - Uneven skin on the neck and chest
            - Fine lines or textural irregularities on the neck or arms

            For sensitive zones like lips and neck, apply with light pressure and follow our guided stamping technique.

            However, avoid using the device directly on the eyelids or waterline. We recommend keeping at least 1.5 inches (around 3 cm) away from the eyes to protect delicate skin.

            Every order includes a detailed step-by-step video guide to show you exactly how and where to apply it for best results.

            Can I apply Hyaluronic Acid, moisturizer, or my usual skincare after treatment?

            Yes — but with a few important guidelines. Right after your treatment, your skin is more permeable and sensitive. This means ingredients absorb better, but it also makes your skin more reactive.

            Here’s what you can use:

            - Hyaluronic acid or hydrating moisturizers: apply in the evening after treatment to boost hydration and support healing
            - Fragrance-free, calming creams (e.g., with panthenol, ceramides, or Centella) are also recommended

            Here’s what to avoid for 24–48 hours:

            - Retinol (Vitamin A)
            - Exfoliating acids (glycolic, lactic, salicylic)
            - Vitamin C serums, scrubs, or strong actives
            - Any skincare with fragrance or alcohol

            Do I need to clean the device? Can I reuse the needle heads?

            The sterile needle heads are single-use only — just like in professional clinics. Reuse can blunt the needles and increase infection risk, so always discard after one treatment. The device body can be rinsed with gentle soap and water, then air-dried before storing.

            Can I use my own serum with the device?

            Please keep in mind that not all serums are suitable for Micro-Infusion. Some can cause irritation, clog the device, or even damage your skin when infused too deeply. That’s why every ELIXA kit comes with our Signature PDRN Formula, developed specifically for safe and effective micro-infusion. It’s sterile, fragrance-free, and dermatologist-formulated to minimize irritation while maximizing collagen stimulation and scar repair. For best results and safety, we recommend using only the included ELIXA serum with your treatments.

            Once you have used long enough to get desired result, do you need to continue using?

            Once you’ve reached your desired results, you don’t need to use it as often — many switch to a maintenance rhythm, like once a month.

            Regular use helps prolong and maintain the effect over time.

            Can I use it if I've had laser, filler or other treatments?

            Yes, but we suggest waiting at least:
            - 2 weeks after fillers / Botox
            - 4/6 weeks after laser treatments

            And as always — check with your dermatologist if you're under clinical care.
