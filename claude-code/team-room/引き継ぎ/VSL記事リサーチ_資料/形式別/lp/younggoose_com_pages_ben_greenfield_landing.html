why these ingredients matter

What your skin needs to age well, backed by research

As we age, the skin goes through measurable biological shifts:

  • NAD+ levels decline
  • Mitochondrial function slows
  • Oxidative stress increases
  • Recovery from UV or treatments becomes delayed
  • Collagen and elasticity begin to break down

The right topical ingredients, delivered in the right way, can support skin’s energy metabolism, structural integrity, and long-term resilience.

support my skin daily

key ingredients in my protocol

exosomes

Biological messengers that support regeneration

I use exosome-based products to deliver precise signals that activate repair and collagen production. The formula I use — Vampire Exosome® Serum — contains 3 trillion PRP-derived exosomes per bottle, independently tested and quantified.

It helps the skin respond faster, look firmer, and recover more effectively over time.

nad+ apex

Essential for cellular repair and energy metabolism

NAD+ is known for its role in longevity and mitochondrial health. It also plays a critical role in skin barrier function, DNA repair, and post-inflammatory recovery.

I use Young Goose’s NAD+ APEX™ system, found in multiple formulas, to support NAD+ topically in a form the skin can use consistently.

methylene blue

Mitochondrial support and antioxidant defense

Methylene Blue enhances mitochondrial efficiency and reduces oxidative stress. I’ve used it internally for years, and now apply it topically through Blue Peptide Spray — a fast mist I use throughout the day after sun exposure, workouts, flights, or blue light exposure.

spermidine

Supports autophagy and long-term skin renewal

Spermidine supports healthy aging through cellular cleanup and renewal. It helps maintain skin integrity over time.

I apply it at night through Youth Reset, often paired with NAD+ boosters, to help support overnight regeneration and visible skin resilience.
