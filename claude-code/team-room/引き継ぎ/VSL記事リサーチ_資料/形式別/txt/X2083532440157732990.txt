
 One weight. That's it. That's the whole gym. Meet the dumbbell. It's dumb in name only. Curls? Yes. Presses? Yes. Swinging it around dramatically to feel powerful? Also yes. I picked one up on Monday. By Friday, I had a personality. No cables. No confusing pulleys. No guy named Chad hogging the machine for 40 minutes. Just one honest chunk of metal that asks nothing but everything. Rows. Squats. Lunges. That one exercise you saw online and are doing completely wrong. Small enough to hide from guests. Heavy enough to earn respect. Warning. May cause biceps, ego and the sudden urge to carry all the groceries in one trip. The dumbbell. Smarter than it sounds.
